财报周期变化对交易者的影响

Half my personality is built around earnings season. The sweaty anticipation, the position I swore I'd size responsibly and then absolutely did not, the after-hours candle that either makes me feel like a genius or keeps me staring at the ceiling at 3am. And now there's this whole thing floating around about companies maybe only reporting twice a year instead of every quarter. Cut the earnings drops in half, just like that. I genuinely can't tell if this is the best or worst thing that could happen to a person like me. Fewer earnings means fewer chances to blow up my account on a Thursday, which is probably good for my net worth and my remaining friendships. But what am I even doing here if I can't buy weeklies into a print and pretend I understand data center demand?
翻译：我一半的个性都围绕着财报季而建立。那种紧张的期待，我发誓要负责任地建仓但却完全没有的头寸，盘后K线图让我觉得自己像个天才，或者让我凌晨3点盯着天花板。现在有传言说公司可能只一年公布两次财报，而不是每个季度。就这样，财报公布次数减半。我真搞不清楚这对像我这样的人来说是好是坏。财报减少意味着周四爆仓的机会减少，这可能对我的净资产和剩下的人际关系有好处。但如果我不能在财报前买入周度期权并假装我理解数据中心需求，那我在这里到底在干什么？

“逢低买入”策略及其表现

Nothing fancy. No calls here. Just buying shares of great companies when I think they’re temporarily mispriced. I also have a call options sandbox and a foundation of S&P index, bonds and cash. Not smarter than anyone. Just plenty of liquidity and discipline. Wait and buy fear…wait again and trim/sell strength, repeat. Post COVID, it’s been basically that easy. My returns have been 2x the index over the last 6 years with this strategy. That includes my foundational boring portfolio and my cash/bonds. Without that drag, much greater over performance. I have losses too. But not a lot. I only own a few positions at a time. It’s important to me what I buy, and even more important when.
翻译：没什么花哨的。这里没有看涨期权。只是在我认为优秀公司暂时被错误定价时买入其股票。我也有一个看涨期权沙盒以及标普指数、债券和现金的基础配置。我并不比任何人都聪明，只是有充足的流动性和纪律。等待并买入恐惧……再次等待并削减/卖出强势，重复。后疫情时代，这基本上就是这么简单。我的回报率在过去六年里通过这种策略是指数的两倍。这包括我的基础性无聊投资组合和我的现金/债券。如果没有这些拖累，表现会更好。我也有亏损，但不多。我一次只持有少数几个头寸。对我来说，买什么很重要，什么时候买更重要。
