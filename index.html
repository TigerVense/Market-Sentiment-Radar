    Spotify is still being valued like a mature music-streaming app when it’s increasingly becoming the dominant global audio platform. Pricing power is finally showing up, margins have inflected hard, FCF is scaling, and they still have multiple under-monetized levers across ads, audiobooks, video/podcasts, and higher-priced tiers. If revenue keeps compounding while margins move toward mature software/platform levels, earnings can grow WAY faster than the top line. I think the market is still underestimating what normalized earnings power looks like here. My bull case is ~$1,200 within the next year.
    翻译：Spotify仍然被估价为一个成熟的音乐流媒体应用，但它正日益成为全球主导的音频平台。定价权终于显现，利润率已大幅改善，自由现金流正在扩大，并且他们在广告、有声书、视频/播客以及更高价位层级等领域仍有多个未充分变现的增长点。如果收入持续复合增长，同时利润率向成熟软件/平台水平靠拢，那么收益的增长速度将远超营收。我认为市场仍然低估了这里正常的盈利能力。我的牛市目标是在未来一年内达到约1200美元。
  • 3. IREN (Iris Energy Limited)
    IREN moved on the Dell earnings read-through — could have more room IREN was up 7.5% Wednesday and closed at $39.60, right near the high of the day. Volume picked up hard into the close, with roughly 10 million shares trading during the final hour. My guess is this was partly a sympathy move off Dell’s earnings. Dell reported huge AI-server demand and a $95B backlog. IREN has a roughly $1.6B agreement to buy Dell’s Nvidia Blackwell systems, so strong demand for AI infrastructure supports the overall IREN story. This isn’t new revenue for IREN, but it shows the AI data-center buildout is still moving fast. The other interesting part is the short interest. The latest reported numbers show about 94M shares short, or roughly 28% of the float.
    翻译：IREN因戴尔财报的传导效应而上涨——可能还有上涨空间。IREN周三上涨7.5%，收于39.60美元，接近当日高点。收盘前交易量大幅增加，最后小时交易了约1000万股。我猜测这部分是受戴尔财报的同情效应推动。戴尔报告称AI服务器需求巨大，积压订单达950亿美元。IREN与戴尔签订了约16亿美元的协议，购买英伟达Blackwell系统，因此对AI基础设施的强劲需求支持了IREN的整体故事。这对IREN来说不是新的收入，但它表明AI数据中心的建设仍在快速推进。另一个有趣的部分是空头兴趣。最新报告的数据显示，约有9400万股被做空，约占流通股的28%。
