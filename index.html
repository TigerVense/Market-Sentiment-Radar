1. 宏观与市场情绪

Every crisis for 17+ years (2008, COVID) got "solved" the same way: cut rates, print money. Never: fix who actually owns the assets. That's the money-centric trap, treating a real resource-distribution problem like it's just a number on a screen, which is money.
翻译：过去17年（2008年、新冠疫情）的每一次危机都以同样的方式“解决”：降息、印钞。从未真正解决资产所有权问题。这是一种以货币为中心的陷阱，将实际的资源分配问题视为屏幕上的数字，即金钱。
Government debt sustainability comes down to interest rate (R) vs inflation + growth (I+G). If R stays above I+G, debt compounds on its own.
翻译：政府债务的可持续性取决于利率（R）与通货膨胀+增长（I+G）的关系。如果R持续高于I+G，债务将自行累积。
Taxing working people more or cutting spending doesn't fix this, both drag down growth (G), making the ratio worse.
翻译：对工薪阶层增税或削减开支都无法解决这个问题，两者都会拖累增长（G），从而使比率恶化。
A wealth tax is one of the few things that actually improves the ratio without also killing growth, because it pulls from money sitting in assets, not money circulating in the economy. My prediction: we won't get the wealth tax. We'll get a rate cut instead.
翻译：财富税是少数几种在不扼杀增长的情况下实际改善这一比率的措施之一，因为它从闲置资产中抽取资金，而不是从经济流通中的资金中抽取。我的预测是：我们不会征收财富税。我们会得到降息。
It seems like each time we get to NVIDIA earnings, it seems like a make or break moment for the entire market.
翻译：NVIDIA每次发布财报时，都似乎是整个市场的关键时刻。
I get it's the largest market cap and it's the AI trade, but it feels like we are one weaker than expected forecast/ earnings call from a possible correction.
翻译：我明白它是市值最大的公司，也是AI交易的代表，但感觉我们距离一次可能的调整，可能只差一份不及预期的预测或财报电话会议。
With Jackson hole coming up, mid term elections, ongoing issues with Iran, tariffs with Canada, rising 10y and 30y and September seasonality. Are we headed for a market correction? Or does NVIDIA just smash and we take off to record highs again?
翻译：随着杰克逊霍尔会议临近，中期选举，伊朗问题持续，与加拿大的关税，10年期和30年期国债收益率上升以及九月的季节性因素。我们正走向市场调整吗？还是NVIDIA会再次大涨，我们再次创下历史新高？
Average S&P 500 CEO pay last year was $22.8M. That’s the AFL-CIO number, and it excludes Musk’s $158B Tesla package because that thing breaks every average it touches.

Multiply $22.8M across 500 companies and you get somewhere around $11B in total CEO pay. The index is worth about $67 trillion. So if every S&P 500 CEO worked for free starting tomorrow, you’d pick up less than 2 basis points.
