    Krispy Kreme ($DNUT) is a struggling business executing a turnaround. It is aiming to expand margins, improve ROIC, pay down debt through refranchising, and pursue growth in its US business through high margin outlets. The plan was announced in early 2025, and since then the company has delivered increasing EBITDA margins, increasing per door revenue, and positive free cash flow (FCF) three quarters in a row.
